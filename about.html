The shop

Gustavo Gallegos, owner.

Owner · Founder · Contractor. Gustavo founded JR Landscapes and is the person behind the company’s reputation.

With years of experience in construction and landscaping, and a true passion for the work, Gustavo is building a name for the company and a portfolio. The secret behind it: an enthusiastic approach to every new project, a focus on details, and open communication with the client.

He personally guides clients through planning, designing, and constructing, making sure they understand what will happen every step of the way and have input into the details. The end result is immaculate delivery of the final product — repeat clients, referrals, and several new friends. Most importantly, another piece for the portfolio.
